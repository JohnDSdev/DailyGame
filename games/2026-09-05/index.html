Beat Bench

Each steel reed is slightly out of tune, and the correction point is different on every setup. Tap a reed, drag its brass tuning weight, then press compare. For 1.6 seconds the bench plays your reed against the reference. Fast beating means the pitches are apart; slow beating means they are close. Tune the whole bank inside the job's tolerance, then ask for inspection.

Mothlight

Eight lantern posts surround the garden. Tap one and every moth moves at once: amber moths take one diagonal-or-straight step toward the light, blue moths take the same kind of step away from it. A moth at the garden edge cannot leave. Put every moth inside the ring with its own number. You may use a lantern more than once.

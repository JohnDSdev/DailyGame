Daily Game 014 / alternating-stitch puzzle

Bobbin Run

One strand, no lifting the needle. Start at the gold bobbin and sew every marked connection exactly once. Red solid lines must be taken while the needle is on the front of the cloth; blue dotted lines must be taken from the back. Every stitch flips the needle to the other side, so choosing a legal line now can strand you later.

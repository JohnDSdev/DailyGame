Daily Game 013 / railway interlocking

Interlock

You have three turnouts and no brake lever. Each train carries the letter of its platform. Tap a turnout to change the route before the locomotive reaches its locking circle. A turnout locks while a train is close, so late taps do nothing. Route every train correctly; the board remembers your fewest switch flips.

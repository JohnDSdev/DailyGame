Daily Game 008 / light geometry

LONG
SHADOW

A lamp hangs over a row of cut-paper figures. Move the lamp and their shadows slide across the stage floor. Each shadow has one numbered brass mark. Line every tip up at once, then hold still long enough for the stagehand to lock the lamp.

The shadows are geometric projections, not hand-authored animations. Later rooms narrow the marks and add more figures.

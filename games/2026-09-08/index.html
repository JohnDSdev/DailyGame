Lockwater

A lock flight has no pumps and no free water. Swipe horizontally from one chamber into its neighbor to move one unit of water across the dividing wall. Tap a gate when the water on both sides is level, and the boat can pass. Get the boat to the right-hand basin using as few transfers as you can.

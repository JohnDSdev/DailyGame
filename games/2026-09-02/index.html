Daily Game 012 / switchboard arcade

Night Exchange

You are alone at a manual telephone exchange. Calls light up on the left with the subscriber they want. Drag a cord from that incoming jack to the matching jack on the right. After the conversation ends, its lamp turns green; tap the left plug to pull the cord before somebody else needs that subscriber. Three dropped calls still passes a shift. Four does not.

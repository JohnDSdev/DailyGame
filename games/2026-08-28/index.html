High Water

Build the smallest levee you can before the river rises. Drag across open ground to place or remove sandbags. When you release the water, it spreads one cell at a time around ridges and levees. Red houses must stay dry. Every map gives you a tight sandbag allowance, so the job is to find the few cells that disconnect the flood from the neighborhood.

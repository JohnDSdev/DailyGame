Ringfall

Three steel rings surround a catch cup. Each ring has one red gate. Drag directly around a ring to index it one notch at a time. Any numbered bearing lined up with an open gate drops inward immediately. Feed the cup in numerical order. If a later bearing reaches the cup first, the mechanism jams.

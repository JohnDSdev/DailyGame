Every route you record comes back as an echo on the next take. Your echoes repeat the same moves while you make a new route. Park them on pressure plates, use them to open doors, and build a path through the level one version of yourself at a time.

MOVE — swipe, arrows, or WASD
WAIT — space or the WAIT button
RECORD — Enter or RECORD ECHO
UNDO — Z or the UNDO button

Matching lowercase plates open uppercase doors. Numbered cells teleport. Later, ! barriers let you pass but stop echoes.
